Privacy Policy – EduPDF

Last Updated: 18 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EduPDF, an educational application designed to help students access educational PDFs and learning materials. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and how users can request its correction or deletion.

By using EduPDF, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address and account information.
  • Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. The app owner should not store passwords in plain text.
  • Profile photo, if provided.
  • Class and educational information, such as Class 9 or Class 10.
  • App preferences, including available theme and notification settings.
  • Technical and usage information processed by service providers.
  • Purchase information, if paid-course features are enabled.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Authenticate users and help protect accounts.
  • Display class-specific PDFs and educational content.
  • Store and display profile photos and learning materials.
  • Manage courses, banners and app updates.
  • Send notifications when enabled.
  • Maintain security and troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Third-Party Services

Firebase by Google: Used for authentication and database services where configured.

Firebase Privacy Information

Cloudinary: Used for storing and delivering PDFs, profile photos, thumbnails and other media where configured.

Cloudinary Privacy Policy

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. The app owner must configure access permissions appropriately.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use third-party cloud services to store information required for app functionality. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell students' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or disclosed where required by law, to protect users, investigate misuse or maintain application security. Student information should not be publicly accessible without appropriate authorization.

7. Notifications

When notifications are enabled, EduPDF may display information about new courses, educational materials or app updates. Users can control available preferences in the app and device notification settings.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained while an account is active or as necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security.

Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information by contacting the app administrator. Associated profile records and uploaded files should also be addressed where applicable.

Deleting the app from a device may not automatically delete information stored in cloud services.

9. Children's Privacy

EduPDF may be used by school students, including children. The app owner should obtain parental or guardian consent where required by applicable law and collect only information necessary to provide educational services. Parents or guardians may contact the administrator regarding a child's information.

10. User Responsibilities

Users should provide accurate information, keep login credentials confidential and avoid uploading private or third-party information without appropriate permission.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.
